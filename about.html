About me

I have a computer engineering background — and for a long time, I thought that was my path. But during my master's, a UX class changed everything. I realised I cared far more about how things feel to use than how they're built under the hood. Even when I was coding, I always gravitated toward frontend and user satisfaction, turns out I was always a UX designer, I just didn't have the name for it yet.

Landing my first internship at Emarsys as a UX Designer felt like confirmation. I completed the MOME Digital Product Design course to deepen my understanding of the process and develop my own projects, and I haven't looked back since.

My engineering background turned out to be one of my biggest assets. I speak developer, I understand technical constraints, I know what's feasible, and I can have real conversations with engineers without losing something in translation. It makes collaboration faster, and it means my designs are grounded in reality from the start.

What I do when I'm not working

If you can't find me at my computer, I'm probably in the kitchen experimenting with a new recipe, planning my next trip, or deep in a crafting project. Baking is my favourite way to decompress, there's something very satisfying about following a process and ending up with something delicious. Turns out I like systems whether I'm at work or not.

I also love to travel and experience new places through food, from food festivals closer to home to exploring local markets and restaurants abroad. I also spend a lot of time reading, going on long walks, and being thoroughly supervised by my cats.
